## Clément Dumeril

MEng student in Control of Robotic and Autonomous Systems at UC Berkeley (May 2027); capstone at the Agile Robotics and Perception Lab (Prof. Giuseppe Loianno). I work on model-based control and learned dynamics models for robots.

- [Fixed-Wing](https://github.com/clementdumeril/Fixed-Wing): fixed-wing simulator where SAC policies fly the control surfaces; in wind, the best of 3 seeds completes 99 of 100 test flights, a PID 97.
- [turtlebot-nmpc](https://github.com/clementdumeril/turtlebot-nmpc): acados NMPC that predicts lidar-tracked obstacles 3 s ahead; in Webots it avoided a person crossing at 0.5 m/s in 6 of 7 runs, Nav2 MPPI in none of 3.
- [WingJEPA](https://github.com/clementdumeril/WingJEPA): latent world models of a fixed-wing UAV; in calm air, a physics head predicts open loop within tolerance for a median 11.4 s, plain JEPA 1.9 s.
- [GeneticWing](https://github.com/clementdumeril/GeneticWing): flatness-based soaring trajectory planning in simulation, with a flow-matching student that plans in 30 to 50 ms.
- [ur-pen-signature](https://github.com/clementdumeril/ur-pen-signature): a UR7e grasps a pen with residual PPO and signs with force control, in MuJoCo: 84% grasp success under camera error, against 74% scripted.
- [PIXIE](https://github.com/clementdumeril/PIXIE): reimplementation of PIXIE (6D pose from one RGB photo and a CAD model), plus a per-part assembly check.

Also: [quadrotor-ros2](https://github.com/clementdumeril/quadrotor-ros2), [ur5e-neural-kinematics](https://github.com/clementdumeril/ur5e-neural-kinematics).
